# Connector Architecture

> **Superseded 2026-09-13.** This page described a self-hosted-per-pod Nango
> (`NangoConnector` talking to a pod-local Nango container over `eve-network`,
> `POST /api/connectors/nango-webhook`, `runNangoSync()`) and a `NangoProvider`
> feed-pipeline bridge. None of that exists anymore: the Control Plane is the sole
> Nango broker (pods call `POST /api/connector-broker` with a relay JWT and hold no
> Nango key unless `SYNAP_CONNECTOR_BROKER=local`), the Nango webhook is verified by
> HMAC on the CP and pokes the pod at `POST /api/connectors/sync-trigger`, and the
> one sync door — `runConnectionSync` (`services/event-sync/connection-sync.ts`) —
> runs from pg-boss queues. `packages/feed-service/src/providers/NangoProvider.ts`
> is deleted.
>
> For the current architecture and CRM-facing behavior, see
> [Connectors](../crm/connectors.mdx). For how to add a new provider's sync kinds,
> see [Adding a sync source](./adding-a-sync-source.mdx).
