Relay is the Synap app for iOS and Android. It is built for supervision: short, frequent acts you do wherever you
are. Approve or reject what your agents propose, capture something before you forget it, and see what your agents
and rules did on your behalf. Long authoring work belongs in [Browser](/docs/use/browser).

## The tab bar

| Tab | The question it answers |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Does anything need me? Pending approvals, and a "See all activity" link to what was done. |
| **Work** | How is the work going? Your open tasks and work sessions. |
| **+** (centre) | Here is a new thing: search, capture or ask. Hold a choice to record your voice. |
| **Data** | What do I have? Browse your records by kind. |
| **Settings** | Configure Relay, including the trust rules your agents work under. |

## Pairing Relay with your pod

`synap init` (from `@synap-core/cli`) ends by showing a pairing link and QR code when your pod is served over HTTPS.
Open it on your phone to connect Relay to that pod. A pod on `localhost` or plain HTTP cannot be paired; run
`synap init --pod-url https://…` again once it has a public HTTPS address.

## Capturing

- **Share sheet**: send links, text and media to your pod from other apps.
- **Capture button**: the centre button of the tab bar.
- **Voice**: hold a capture choice to dictate.

## Permissions

Relay asks for a permission only for the feature that needs it: contacts (to import them as people), microphone and
speech recognition (voice capture), camera (business cards and photos), and the photo library (photos you attach, and
photo import if you turn it on).

Relay is not open source.
