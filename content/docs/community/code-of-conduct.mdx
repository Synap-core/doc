Synap's community spaces (the GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests and discussions, and the Discord server)
follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory language or unwelcome sexual attention, in public or in private.
- Do not publish other people's private information without their permission.
- Keep feedback specific and about the work.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, or you see a breach of this code, email
**hello@synap.live**. Reports are handled privately. Tell us what happened, where, and who was involved, with links
if you have them.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may ban people who break it
repeatedly or seriously.

This page is a draft: a `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` in each repository will become the canonical text.
