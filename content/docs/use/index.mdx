Every app talks to the same pod. Pick by what you are doing, not by which app has the most features.

| App | Where | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| [Browser](/docs/use/browser) | macOS, Windows, Linux | The desktop workbench: views, documents, whiteboards, schema and long reviews. |
| [Relay](/docs/use/relay) | iOS, Android | Supervision on the go: approve what your agents propose, capture things, see how the work is going. |
| [Raycast](/docs/use/raycast) | macOS (Raycast) | Quick reads and writes from the Raycast launcher. |
| [Eve](/docs/use/eve) | Your server | An operator tool that can run a Synap pod as part of a larger self-hosted stack. |

Your AI agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and others) are not apps in this list: they connect to your pod directly.
See [Connect an agent](/docs/build/agents).

Whether your pod is hosted by Synap or by you does not change how the apps work. See
[Self-hosted pod or Synap Cloud](/docs/use/cloud-vs-self-hosted).
