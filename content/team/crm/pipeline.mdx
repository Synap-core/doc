# Lead-first Pipeline

Pipeline is the CRM's daily workbench. Its cards are people and companies with
the workspace-local `crm-lead` facet—not deals. A lead can be researched and
contacted without a commercial proposal. A deal is an optional proposal linked
to one primary counterparty.

## Lifecycle

```text
crm-lead: new → researching → outreach → engaged → qualified
                                     └→ nurturing | disqualified
deal:     draft → proposed → negotiating → won | lost
won deal: counterparty gains crm-client; lead becomes converted; handoff begins
```

The CRM does not create client-delivery records. A won client remains visible in
the Pipeline's compact **Clients won** section, then moves to the next system.

## Daily interface

- **Next actions** shows due follow-ups and explicit next steps.
- **Lead board** groups active leads by operational stage.
- **Clients won** preserves handoff visibility without becoming a post-sale
  workspace.
- **Contacts** and **Companies** are research directories. They default to CRM
  subjects and expose an intentional All escape hatch.

## Data boundary

The Business Developer template add-on composes onto a CRM workspace. It owns
the workspace-scoped `crm-lead` and `crm-client` facets, commercial deal fields,
commission-entitlement records, and proposal-first playbooks. Pod-wide `lead`
and `client` roles remain generic vocabulary and must not be used as CRM
lifecycle state.

CRM reads generic entity/facet/relation APIs with an explicit workspace lens,
rich facet overlays, and Pod-wide identity inclusion only where a directory
needs it. The backend has no CRM-specific read model.
