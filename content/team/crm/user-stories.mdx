# User Stories

## Personas

### The Salesperson
A team member actively working leads — reaching out, qualifying, booking calls, and moving deals forward. They spend most of their time in the pipeline and contact detail views. They need fast access to the status of every lead, quick note-taking after a call, and clear next actions.

**Core jobs:**
- Know who to follow up with today
- Log a call note and set the next follow-up date in under 30 seconds
- Move a lead from `qualifying` to `call_booked` after a positive signal
- See everything about a contact before jumping on a call

### The Team Lead
Oversees the pipeline, tracks conversion rates, spots stuck deals. Spends time on the home dashboard and pipeline kanban. Needs an at-a-glance view of health across all deals without clicking into each one.

**Core jobs:**
- See the full pipeline state in one view
- Identify leads that have been stuck in a stage too long
- Know which team member owns each lead
- Understand the overall revenue potential in the pipeline

### The Founder / Account Owner
Manages active client relationships — making sure delivery is on track, contracts are in place, and the client is healthy. Primarily uses the client dashboard per company or person.

**Core jobs:**
- Open a client dashboard and immediately see their contract, active tasks, and last activity
- Generate a new contract from a template in one click
- Know if a client is at risk (no recent activity, overdue tasks)
- Access shared documents with the client without leaving the CRM

## User Flows

### 1. Working a lead through the pipeline

```
1. Salesperson opens Pipeline kanban
2. Spots a new lead in the "lead" column
3. Opens contact detail → sees LinkedIn, source, ICP score, last contact date
4. Logs a call note (document editor, auto-linked to contact)
5. Sets crmStatus → "contacted", sets nextFollowUpDate
6. Card moves to "contacted" column automatically
7. Follow-up date appears on home dashboard "needs attention" widget
```

### 2. Qualifying and booking a call

```
1. Salesperson receives a reply from a contacted lead
2. Opens contact detail → reviews previous notes
3. Updates estimatedValue, icpScore after qualification conversation
4. Sets crmStatus → "qualifying", then → "call_booked"
5. Creates a task "Send calendar invite" linked to this contact
6. Task appears in activity timeline and home dashboard
```

### 3. Closing a client and generating a contract

```
1. Call happens, lead converts
2. Sets crmStatus → "client"
3. Contact detail switches to client dashboard view (different panel layout)
4. Opens Documents section → clicks "New from template"
5. Selects "Service Agreement" template
6. Fills in: client name, legal entity, service scope, start date, monthly fee
7. System generates a new document entity linked to this contact
8. Document is editable, collaborative, versioned
9. Sends link to client (future: via client channel)
```

### 4. Managing an active client

```
1. Founder opens client dashboard for a company
2. Sees: active contract, open tasks, last activity date, revenue figure
3. Notices a task "Deliver Q2 report" is overdue
4. Clicks task → updates it, pings team member
5. Checks document section → opens shared proposal, leaves a comment
6. (Future) Opens client channel → posts update to team thread about this client
```

### 5. Finding a new lead

```
1. Team member opens "Find Leads" surface
2. Searches or browses — currently manual, future AI-assisted discovery
3. Adds a new person manually OR imports from The Arch (manual bridge)
4. Person created as crmStatus: "lead" with source tagged
5. Appears in pipeline kanban "lead" column immediately
```

### 6. Team lead reviewing the pipeline

```
1. Opens Pipeline kanban
2. Scans columns — notices 6 leads stuck in "contacted" for >14 days
3. Filters by age or owner to identify responsible salesperson
4. Flags stuck deals for follow-up in team standup
5. (Future) Automation: if lead stuck in stage >N days → notify owner automatically
```
