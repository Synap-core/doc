# CRM

Synap CRM is a focused pre-sale application: a business developer turns a
person or company into a qualified lead, attaches one or more commercial
proposals, wins a deal, and hands the resulting client to another system.

It is not a delivery, project-management, campaign, document, or automation
application. The visible product is intentionally three surfaces:

- **Pipeline** — daily lead work, follow-ups, and clients won.
- **Contacts** — people research, import, enrichment, and qualification.
- **Companies** — organizational research and qualification.

The reusable **Business Developer** template add-on supplies the CRM-local
facets, commercial proposal fields, commission model, and human-governed
playbooks. Any Pod can compose it onto CRM without creating a second workspace
or altering the generic data backend.
