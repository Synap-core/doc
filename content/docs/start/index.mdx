Synap is a data pod: a server that holds your records and lets the AI agents you connect work on them, with every
agent change reaching your data as a proposal you can review. Read [What is Synap?](/docs/start/what-is-synap) for
the model, then pick your path.

## Not sure yet?

- If you want the shortest path to a working pod, take the [Cloud quickstart](/docs/start/cloud-quickstart).
- If your data must stay on hardware you control, take the [self-host quickstart](/docs/start/self-host-quickstart).
  [Self-hosted pod or Synap Cloud](/docs/use/cloud-vs-self-hosted) compares the two.
- Words you do not know are in the [glossary](/docs/start/glossary).
