# CRM Business Developer Data Model

The base CRM template is deliberately small. The **Business Developer** add-on
composes onto it when a Pod needs a lead-first sales motion.

| Primitive | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Person / Company | Canonical CRM subject and commercial counterparty |
| `crm-lead` facet | Workspace-local lifecycle, next action, qualification, and responsible user |
| Deal | Optional commercial proposal; exactly one primary counterparty |
| `crm-client` facet | Added when a deal is won; carries handoff context only |
| Commission entitlement | Immutable record of 20% of Synap fee for a first win, then 10% for later wins |

`crm-lead` and `crm-client` are workspace-scoped role facets. This is essential:
a person can be a CRM lead in one workspace without becoming a lead throughout
the Pod. Generic shared `lead` and `client` roles remain separate.

## Visibility

Business Developer creates operational people and companies workspace-scoped so
all workspace members can read the same lead. CRM lifecycle facets are attached
with the explicit CRM workspace lens. Pod-wide identities remain owner-private
unless a future generic sharing policy grants access.

Current CRM visibility is deliberately all-visible for workspace members.
Assignment is responsibility and dashboard context, not record-level security.
Assigned-only visibility requires a later platform-wide policy across entities,
relations, search, graph, documents, and Hub APIs.
