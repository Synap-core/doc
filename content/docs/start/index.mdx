Synap is a data pod: a server that holds your records and lets the AI agents you connect work on them. By default an
agent's creates and edits apply directly and can be undone, while deletions and structural changes wait for your
approval. Read [What is Synap?](/docs/concepts/what-is-synap) for the model, then pick your path.

Synap is for people and small teams who work with AI agents and want their notes, contacts, tasks and documents in
one place they own. For example, you ask Claude Code to log a call with a client: it adds the person and a note about the call to
your pod, and you find both in the Synap app on your desktop.
